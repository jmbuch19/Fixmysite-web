## 1. What fixmysite.in does

fixmysite.in scans websites and generates health reports. We check phone numbers, links, SSL certificates, content quality, and other signals — and tell you what we found in plain language.

We are a diagnostic tool. We are not a web development agency. We do not fix your website for you (unless you engage our partner developers separately). We provide information. What you do with that information is your decision.

## 4. Accuracy of reports

We make every effort to provide accurate scan results. However:

- Our phone verification uses Twilio's Lookup API — we verify whether a number is active in telecom records, not whether a human will answer
- Our link checker reflects the status of links at the time of the scan — links change
- Our AI-generated findings are recommendations, not guarantees
- Our health score is a relative indicator, not a certification

**We do not guarantee that fixing every issue we identify will result in increased traffic, sales, or business outcomes.** A healthy website is a necessary condition for online success — not a sufficient one.
