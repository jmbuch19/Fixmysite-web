# About fixmysite.in

## For the business owner reading this

You built something real. A clinic that heals people. A shop that families have trusted for years. A practice that took decades to establish.

Then someone told you: *"You need a website."*

So you got one. And then it sat there — quietly, invisibly — while phone numbers stopped working, contact forms went nowhere, and customers who tried to reach you online simply gave up and called someone else.

You never knew. Because no one told you. And because checking your website was never on your list — you were busy running your business.

**That's the gap we're here to close.**

fixmysite.in reads your website the way your customer does — not as code, not as a developer, but as a person trying to call you, find you, book with you, or trust you. We find what's broken. We tell you in plain language. We tell you exactly what to fix, in what order, and what it will cost.

No jargon. No guesswork. No consultant who talks for three hours and sends a 40-page report you'll never read.

Just the truth about your website — and a clear path forward.
